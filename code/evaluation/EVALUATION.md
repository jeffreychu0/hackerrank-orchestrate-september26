## What these numbers do not show

- **Most provider disagreement is cosmetic.** Of the adjudicated items, 15 had differing claim sets but only 1 had any differing output field. The judges are mostly comparing wording, scope metadata and certainty labels.
- **Adjudication is not correctness.** A preferred reading is one a peer model found better supported by the evidence. On the sample set the two providers produce the same row often enough that there is almost never a case where exactly one was right, so preference cannot be checked against ground truth. That is why section 3 exists.
- **The judges share a blind spot with the producers.** A fact both readings miss and neither judge flags is invisible to this method.
- **The calibration set is small and synthetic.** It measures sensitivity to defects chosen in advance, not to unknown ones.
- **Sample agreement is 25 rows.** Differences of one or two rows are noise, and the remaining gaps there are forecast-threshold effects rather than rule errors - see `code/docs/HARNESS.md`.
